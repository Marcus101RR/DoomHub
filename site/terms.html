5. HOW RULES ARE ENFORCED

Staff (Moderators, Community Managers, Admins and the Super Admin) keep Doom Hub a good place to be.

  • Infractions: breaking these Terms can get you an infraction - a recorded warning. You can see your own infractions on your profile.
  • Automatic bans: infractions within any 12 months lead to automatic bans:

3 infractions: 3-day ban

5 infractions: 5-day ban

7 infractions: 7-day ban

10 infractions: 14-day ban

12 infractions: 30-day ban

13 or more: permanent ban

An infraction stops counting toward automatic bans 12 months after it was given.

  • Staff decisions: for serious cases, staff can mute, kick, or ban an account directly, for any length of time or permanently, without going through infractions first.
  • If you're banned, Doom Hub shows you why, for how long, and your infractions. If you think a decision was wrong, use the appeal contact shown in that window.
